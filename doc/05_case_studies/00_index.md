# Case studies {.unnumbered}

Chromatographic processes vary widely in complexity, ranging from simple batch elution to multi-column continuous systems.
Depending on the separation task, different process configurations, evaluation methods, and optimization strategies are required.
To demonstrate the flexibility of the developed framework, this chapter presents several case studies covering a range of chromatographic processes and operating concepts.

The first case study focuses on the characterization of a real system using experimental data, emphasizing parameter estimation and system modeling.
Subsequent case studies compare a variety of advanced operating concepts, illustrating how different configurations impact separation performance and optimization strategies.

Together, these case studies showcase the framework’s ability to handle diverse chromatographic processes, enabling structured modeling, simulation, and optimization across a wide range of applications.
